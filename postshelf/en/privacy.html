PostShelf Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 30, 2026

The PostShelf developer (the "Developer") has established this Privacy Policy to explain how the iOS app "PostShelf" (the "App") handles users' information.

1. Information the App Handles

The App stores the following information on your device. This information is never sent to the Developer's servers (the Developer does not operate any servers that collect users' information).

  • Information about saved posts (post URL, text, author's display name and username, URLs of profile images, attached images, and video thumbnails, posting date and time, and information about quoted posts)
  • Shelves and tags you create, along with their colors and order
  • Settings such as appearance and theme color

If iCloud sync provided by Apple is enabled in the future, the information above will be synced between devices signed in with the same Apple Account through your own iCloud account. This sync is performed by Apple's services, and the Developer cannot see its contents.

2. Use of External Services to Retrieve Post Content

When you save a post to the App from the Share button in the X app, the App sends the URL of the shared post (the username and post ID) to an external service, "FxTwitter" (api.fxtwitter.com), to retrieve the content of the post. FxTwitter is not an official service of X Corp.; it is provided by a third party.

In addition, to display images and video thumbnails of saved posts, the App loads images from image delivery servers provided by X Corp. (such as pbs.twimg.com). As with ordinary web browsing, information such as your device's IP address is sent to these servers.

3. Advertising

The App displays ads using "Google AdMob," an ad delivery service provided by Google LLC. To display ads and measure their effectiveness, Google AdMob may collect the following information:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow the app to track you
  • Device information such as IP address, device model, OS version, and language settings
  • Usage information such as how many times ads are shown and whether they are tapped

Ads are still shown if you do not allow tracking, but they will not be tailored to your interests (personalized ads). You can change your tracking permission anytime in the iPhone Settings app under Privacy & Security > Tracking.

For how Google handles information, please see Google's Privacy Policy and Google's advertising policies.

4. List of Information Sent Externally

In accordance with Japan's Telecommunications Business Act, the information sent from the App to external parties is disclosed below.

5. Purchases

When you purchase paid features of the App, payment is processed through Apple's App Store. The Developer never receives payment information such as credit card numbers.

6. Sharing with Third Parties

Except as described in sections 2 to 4 above, the Developer does not share users' information with third parties.

7. Deleting Your Information

You can delete the information saved in the App by choosing Settings > Delete All Posts in the App, or by deleting the App from your device.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

The Developer may change this Policy as necessary. Changes will be published on this page.

This Policy is a translation of the original Japanese version. If there is any discrepancy between the Japanese and English versions, the Japanese version prevails.
